# Code of Conduct

`qlib-platform` is an engineering and research project. We expect collaboration to be technically rigorous, evidence-driven, and professionally respectful.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-managed chat spaces, events, and situations where someone is acting as a representative of the project.

## Our commitment

We want contributors, users, reviewers, and maintainers to be able to participate without harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.

Technical disagreement is expected. Personal hostility is not.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- discuss ideas, code, research methodology, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking people;
- give review feedback that is specific, actionable, and proportionate to the risk of the change;
- receive constructive criticism professionally and correct mistakes when evidence shows a problem;
- distinguish facts, assumptions, hypotheses, benchmarks, and opinions;
- respect privacy, confidential information, credentials, account identifiers, proprietary datasets, and embargoed security reports;
- avoid presenting exploratory backtests or model results as guarantees of future investment performance;
- preserve the project's research-governance boundaries, including sealed holdouts and research/execution separation;
- disclose material conflicts of interest when they could affect technical or research judgment;
- help keep issues and pull requests focused on the project rather than unrelated personal, political, or commercial disputes.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained unwanted attention;
- insults, slurs, degrading comments, or identity-based discrimination;
- sexualized language or imagery in project spaces;
- deliberate intimidation or disruption of collaboration;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- knowingly exposing secrets, credentials, broker/account identifiers, or private vulnerability details;
- fabricating research evidence, falsifying benchmark results, manipulating lineage records, or misrepresenting review/certification status;
- pressuring maintainers or contributors to bypass safety, governance, licensing, or security controls;
- retaliation against people who raise good-faith technical, research-integrity, security, or conduct concerns.

## Technical disagreement

Strong technical criticism is welcome when it stays focused on the work. Examples of appropriate disagreement include challenging a causal assumption, identifying look-ahead bias, rejecting an unsafe migration, disputing a benchmark methodology, or requesting stronger evidence for a performance claim.

Reviewers should explain the failure mode or invariant at risk whenever practical. Contributors should respond to the substance of that feedback rather than treating review as a personal judgment.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy. Depending on severity and history, responses may include:

1. a private or public clarification of expected behavior;
2. a warning and request to correct or stop the behavior;
3. temporary restrictions on participation;
4. removal of comments, issues, pull requests, or other material that violates this policy;
5. a permanent ban from project-managed community spaces for severe or repeated violations.

Serious threats, doxxing, credential exposure, or security-sensitive disclosures may be handled immediately without following every intermediate step.

Maintainers should apply enforcement consistently, document material decisions where appropriate, and protect reporter privacy when disclosure would create additional risk.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through an appropriate GitHub channel rather than escalating the dispute in a public thread.

For vulnerabilities, leaked credentials, integrity bypasses, or other security-sensitive matters, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Do not place sensitive personal information, secrets, broker/account identifiers, or exploit details in a public issue.
